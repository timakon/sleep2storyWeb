## Evidence limits and preserved boundaries

These were three focused search formulations per language through the live web search tool, followed by opening the four cited native-language pages. The search tool did not provide a verified France/Spain location setting, query volumes, keyword difficulty, Search Console data or stable ranks. Results in Spanish also included broad personal-reading and non-Spain material; those were not used as the basis for Spain-specific wording. Exact-query popularity is unverified for both languages. Related queries are editorial candidates, not five separately measured keyword opportunities.

For voice-care accuracy, the primary [NIDCD — Taking Care of Your Voice](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taking-care-your-voice) page was opened separately. Its guidance supports resting a hoarse or tired voice and avoiding whispering. The localized article preserves the English source’s limited medical scope and its request to seek advice for worrying or persistent symptoms; it adds no diagnosis or treatment.

Both localizations retain four immediate alternatives, original example phrases for children and a three-step plan to prepare recordings on a better day. They retain the product’s prerelease status, planned English/Russian narration only, complete story-text input, consent and synthetic family-voice narration. They do not claim French/Spanish narration, translation, offline playback, export availability or guaranteed child sleep. Playback checks refer to the reader’s chosen audio player, not to promised Sleep2Story features. All metadata, captions, dates, source labels and the “English” source-language marker are translated.
