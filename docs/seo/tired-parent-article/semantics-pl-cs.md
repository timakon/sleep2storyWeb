# Polish and Czech search wording

Checked on 7 September 2026. This is a small live wording check, not keyword-volume research. The proposed queries below are editorial choices based on the article's intent and observed local language. They are not claims about measured search demand, rankings, difficulty or conversion.

## Polish

Primary query: **nie mam siły czytać bajki**.

Secondary queries:
- zmęczeni rodzice czytanie bajek
- ból gardła czytanie dziecku
- bajki na dobranoc do słuchania
- nagrywanie bajki na dobranoc

Exact search formulations used:
1. `"nie mam siły" "czytać" "bajki"`
2. `"czytanie bajek" "zmęczeni rodzice"`
3. `"bajki na dobranoc" "nagranie" "głos"`

Relevant returned sources:
- [Newsweek: Rodzicielstwo pod napięciem](https://www.newsweek.pl/psychologia/rodzicielstwo-pod-napieciem-nawet-bajki-czytam-dziecku-w-stresie/8dre1hl) uses everyday language about tired parents and reading to a child. The search excerpt was used for wording only; no health or parenting-outcome claims were taken from it.
- [Bajka na dobranoc: bajki do słuchania](https://bajkanadobranoc.com/do-sluchania/krotkie-bajki/) shows the natural content label “bajki do słuchania”. Its sleep-related marketing claims were not adopted.
- [bajka.app](https://bajka.app/) uses the local vocabulary of a voice sample, bedtime story and evening fatigue. This competing product is only a vocabulary reference; its availability, features and numerical claims do not describe Sleep2Story.

Choice: the title uses “Brak siły”, and the H1 addresses the parent with “Nie masz już siły…”. This avoids a gendered adjective in the main question. The body alternates ordinary terms such as “bajka”, “nagranie” and “oglądanie obrazków” according to meaning. It does not repeat the full target phrase in every heading. “Ból gardła” remains a practical reason to rest the voice, without turning the page into treatment advice.

Limit: the tightly quoted fatigue search also returned unrelated reading discussions and book excerpts. It supports a natural formulation, not proof that this exact long-tail query has significant demand.

## Czech

Primary query: **nemám sílu číst pohádku**.

Secondary queries:
- únava při čtení pohádky
- bolest v krku čtení dětem
- pohádky na dobrou noc k poslechu
- nahrát pohádku na dobrou noc

Exact search formulations used:
1. `"unavení rodiče" "pohádky"`
2. `"čtení pohádky" "únava"`
3. `"pohádky na dobrou noc" "nahrávka"`

Relevant returned sources:
- [National Library of the Czech Republic: Pohádky v životě dětí](https://ipk.nkp.cz/docs/nkcr_bookstart_2024_e_kniha.pdf) includes Czech discussion of tired parents, reading and recorded stories. The search excerpt informed vocabulary; it is not used to claim identical benefits of audio and shared reading.
- [Starostlivý táta: Krátké pohádky pro unavené rodiče](https://starostlivytata.cz/?p=143) illustrates ordinary Czech phrasing and the overlap with a specific book title.
- [Knihy Dobrovský: Krátké pohádky pro unavené rodiče](https://www.knihydobrovsky.cz/kniha/kratke-pohadky-pro-unavene-rodice-451116616) confirms that overlap in book-commerce results.
- [Supraphonline: Buddhovy pohádky na dobrou noc](https://www.supraphonline.cz/album/166317-nagaraja-buddhovy-pohadky/cd?trackId=1981827) supplies established Czech audio vocabulary, including “nahrávka” and “pohádky na dobrou noc”.

Choice: lead with “Nemáte už sílu číst pohádku na dobrou noc?” to express the parent's immediate situation. Avoid making “pohádky pro unavené rodiče” the main target because the observed result set prominently features Michal Viewegh's book. Use “pohádka k poslechu”, “nahrávka”, “šetřit hlas” and “prohlížet si obrázky” where each fits. The article uses consistent polite plural address and locally natural date formatting.

Limit: these results are a qualitative sample. They do not show how often Czech parents search any proposed query. The medical secondary query was selected for content coverage, not proven traffic potential.

## Shared factual boundaries

- [NIDCD: Taking Care of Your Voice](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taking-care-your-voice), opened during this pass, recommends resting a hoarse or tired voice and avoiding whispering as an extreme of voice use. Both versions retain that narrow point and mark the linked guidance as English.
- Both articles preserve the same complete sections, five FAQs, captions, metadata and 7 September 2026 date as the English source. They inherit the existing brand-image alt text instead of adding a conflicting override.
- Product copy says the iOS/Android app is in development; launch narration is planned only in English and Russian. It requires the full story text and a short voice sample used with the speaker's consent to prepare synthetic narration. The translations make no claim about Polish/Czech narration, fourteen narration languages, offline playback, export, story writing, treatment or guaranteed sleep.
